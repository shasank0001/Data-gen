# Research plan: persona terms

## Question

How should skepticism, credulity (credulousness), and gullibility be distinguished when specifying short system prompts and model responses?

## Subtopics

1. Dictionary definitions of skepticism and their emphasis on evidence and doubt.
2. Dictionary definitions of credulity/credulousness and gullibility, including their relationship.
3. Applied distinction for the proposed dataset, avoiding the mistake of treating a credulous persona as simply a gullible assistant.

## Synthesis

Use authoritative dictionaries as primary sources and academic material only if needed for nuance. Produce concise implementation guidance with source URLs.
